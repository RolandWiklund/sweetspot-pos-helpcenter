Get started

Install and sign in for the first time

Sweetspot POS runs on iPad or iPhone. Follow the steps below the first time you set up a register.

1. Download the app

  1. Open the App Store on your iPad or iPhone.
  2. Search for ExtendaGO POS and install the app. (Sweetspot POS runs on top of the ExtendaGO POS app.)
  3. Wait for the download to finish, then open the app.

2. Sign in

  1. Enter the sign-in credentials you received from Sweetspot when your account was set up.
  2. Pick the correct store (if you have more than one).
  3. Pick the correct register — each iPad is usually tied to a specific register.

3. Pick a cashier and enter the float

  1. Pick the signed-in cashier's name (or enter a PIN code if your setup requires it).
  2. Enter the cash float — the amount in the cash drawer at the start of the day.
  3. Confirm, and you'll land on the sales view.
Tip If the app shows a red status indicator next to the printer or terminal icon in the top menu — go to Settings → Printer or Settings → Terminals and connect the right equipment. See Hardware.

At the POS

Selling products and taking payment

Add products to the receipt

  1. Tap a product in the product grid, or search for it in the search bar. Alternatively — scan the barcode.
  2. If the product has variants (size, color, etc.) — pick the correct variant.
  3. The product is added to the receipt view on the right. Tap it there to change quantity, discount or variant.

Add a manual product

If a customer wants to buy something that isn't in the catalogue, tap manual line and enter a description, price and VAT. Perfect for ad-hoc or unique items.

Take payment

  1. Tap the green Pay button.
  2. Pick a payment method — card, cash, gift card, invoice, MobilePay and more (depending on your setup).
  3. Follow the prompt for the chosen method. For card payments, the amount is sent to the card terminal.
  4. The receipt is printed automatically on the receipt printer once payment is approved.

Park an ongoing sale

If a customer needs to wait (e.g. grab something from the car), tap the Park button. You can now serve the next customer. Pick the parked sale back up from the parked-sales list.

Split a bill

In hospitality/restaurant setups you can split a sale across several people. Tap Share bill and select which lines go on each part.

At the POS

Apply a discount

You can add a discount to a single product or to the entire receipt.

Discount on a single product

  1. Tap the product in the receipt view.
  2. Pick percentage discount, amount discount, or set a new price.
  3. Enter the value and (optionally) a reason for the discount.
  4. Tap OK — the discount appears immediately in the receipt view.

Discount on the whole receipt

  1. Tap the Discount button in the toolbar.
  2. Pick a discount type and value.
  3. The discount is applied to every eligible line.
Good to know If a discount comes from a campaign (marked as Markdown), it can't be edited or removed at the register. Campaigns are managed in the web backoffice.
Receipts

Receipts and receipt copies

Reprint a receipt for an earlier sale

The most common way to get a receipt copy directly from the register — for example when a customer calls in asking for a receipt from a past purchase.

  1. Tap the Backoffice button in the top-left corner of the sales view.
  2. Go to Sale in the menu on the left.
  3. Pick the Orders tab. All completed sales are listed, sorted by date. Use the Date button in the top right to filter by period.
  4. Tap the order you want to print — you'll see what was sold and how it was paid.
  5. Tap the printer icon to the right of the order to print a receipt copy, or the envelope icon (Order) to email the receipt.
Tip If the printer icon doesn't appear — check that the cashier's role (Management / Owner / Staff) has permission to view sales history under Users → Permissions.

Send the receipt by email or SMS

At the payment screen you can enter an email address or phone number before completing the sale — the receipt is then sent digitally. The same can be done after the fact via Backoffice → Sale → Orders, as above.

Pro forma receipt

Need to print a preliminary receipt (e.g. for a restaurant table that hasn't paid yet)? Use the Print bill button. This is a supporting document only — not a receipt.

Cached receipts (printer offline)

If the receipt printer is offline when a sale is closed, the receipt is saved to a print queue. Once the printer is back, a red badge appears on the printer icon — tap the icon and choose Print cached receipts to print them.

At the POS

Returns and refunds

Return against a receipt (recommended)

It's always best to process a return against the customer's original receipt — that way the correct price and VAT rate are filled in automatically.

  1. Tap the Return button (products in the receipt view now appear in red = return mode).
  2. Scan the QR code on the customer's receipt, or type in the receipt number manually.
  3. Pick the lines to return.
  4. Fill in the Reason for return.
  5. Tap Tender and pick how the customer should be refunded (the same payment method as the original purchase is the usual default).

Manual return (no receipt)

Only do this if you can't locate the customer's original receipt:

  1. Tap Return.
  2. Add the product being returned — or tap manual line if the product isn't in the catalogue.
  3. Enter a reason for the return.
  4. Complete the refund payment (cash or refund to card).
Important While in return mode, all prices show as negative. Tap the Return button again to exit return mode before starting a new regular sale.
At the POS

End of Day (EOD)

At the end of the working day you reconcile the register — count cash, bank the deposit and confirm the totals.

  1. Tap the EOD button in the sales view.
  2. Count the cash in the drawer and enter the amount in Cash in register.
  3. Enter the amount going to the bank under Deposit to bank (any float you leave behind is deducted from the deposit).
  4. Confirm the card payment total from the terminal's end-of-day report.
  5. Tap Confirm — the system calculates whether it's a balance match or balance mismatch.
Tip On a balance mismatch — double-check your count, and look for any untracked cash withdrawals or deposits during the day. The system logs the difference for audit purposes.

X-report (snapshot during the day)

You can pull an X-report at any time via Backoffice → Reports. It shows sales, returns, discounts and cash status up to the current moment without closing the day.

Navigating to Backoffice

  1. Tap the Backoffice button in the top-left corner of the sales view.
  2. You land on a menu view with every admin function: Home, Sale, KPI, Inventory, Stock, Reports, Users, Settings.
  3. Tap the Sales window button at the top to go back to the register.
Admin

Products and categories

Add a new product

  1. Go to Backoffice → Inventory → Products and tap the plus icon (+).
  2. Enter a name, (optional) description, purchase price excl. VAT and sales price incl. VAT.
  3. Enter the stock level.
  4. (Optional) Add variants — e.g. size or color.
  5. (Optional) Assign the product to one or more categories under Add categories. Without a category it ends up under Other.
  6. (Optional) Add a product image.
  7. Tap Add product.

Edit or delete a product

Tap the pencil icon to the right of a product to open edit mode. When done — tap Edit product. To delete the product entirely, tap the red Remove button.

Note When you delete a product, it disappears from every store that had it in the catalogue. If the product has sales history, we recommend archiving it instead (remove it from the shelf category but keep the underlying record).

Categories

Create categories via Backoffice → Inventory → Categories → Add category. Categories can be multi-level — pick an existing category as the Top-category when creating a sub-category.

Admin

Stock management

Add to stock

  1. Go to Backoffice → Stock → Add to stock.
  2. Tap the plus icon, pick a category and product.
  3. Tap the pencil icon and enter the quantity to add.
  4. Confirm. You can enter a Reference ID and description (e.g. a supplier invoice number).

Remove from stock

Same procedure under the Remove from stock tab. Use it for shrinkage or internal consumption.

Rolling count (single item)

Under Count stock you can count a single product and set the actual balance. Good for ongoing spot checks during the day.

Full inventory count

  1. Go to Stocks → Stock in the GO CTRL app and pick the right stock location.
  2. Go to Inventory counts and tap Open a new, full item count.
  3. Pick store and currency, then tap Create item count.
  4. Each team member signs in on their device and picks Full items count → Participate in a full item count.
  5. Split responsibility between shelves/areas so everything gets counted exactly once.
  6. Scan or search each article and enter the quantity.
  7. When everyone is done — swipe left on the count and tap Send, confirm with Submit and close.
  8. Open the web backoffice on PC → Item count → Products. Check counted vs. uncounted products, and adjust manually if needed.
  9. Tap Update item count, then go to the Details tab and tap Close.
Note Counting while the store is open isn't supported by the automation. If you count mid-day, you'll need to follow up with a rolling count and pull a sales report for the period.
Admin

Users and permissions

Manage who can do what at the register. There are three roles by default: Management, Owner and Staff.

Add a user

  1. Go to Backoffice → Users → Add user.
  2. Enter a name and email address.
  3. Pick a role via Attach role.
  4. Recommended: set a PIN code for quick sign-in at the register.
  5. (Optional) Add a profile picture.
  6. Tap Add user.

Edit what a role can do

Go to Users → Permission. Here you can, for example, set Staff so they can't process returns, view sales history, or edit products.

Tip Cashiers who often get asked for receipt copies need permission to view Orders/Sales history. Check this first when troubleshooting.
Admin

Reports

X-report

An X-report is a snapshot of the day's sales and cash status without closing the day. Includes among other things:

  • Products sold
  • Products returned
  • Cash drawer openings
  • Cash in register
  • Discount totals

Pulled via Backoffice → Reports → X-reports and can be printed on the receipt printer.

Sales stats

Under Home → Sale you'll find revenue per hour, day, week and month as charts. Filter by any period with the Date button.

KPI

Under KPI you'll find the average basket size and margin per product — handy for quickly spotting what's making the most money.

Hardware

Recommended hardware

Sweetspot POS works with a wide range of equipment. Here's what we recommend — reach out to support if you're unsure what fits your setup.

iPad and iPhone

iPad

iPadOS 13 or later

All modern iPads work — a newer model gives the best performance, especially when scanning and taking payment simultaneously.

iPhone

iOS 13 or later

Used for mobile POS and for the ExtendaGO CTRL app (stock, counts).

Receipt printers

Star mC-Print3

LAN + USB hub

The standard choice. Supports cash drawer, scanner and customer display from a single unit.

Star mPOP

Printer + cash drawer

Combined unit. Compact option for smaller registers.

Epson TM-T88VI

BT / LAN / WiFi

Fast printer (300mm/s) for high-volume stores.

Star TSP143IIIU

USB

Budget-friendly — doesn't support every scanner though.

Seiko RP-F10

USB / USB-C / BT

Compact design, IPx1 splash rating on some models.

Card terminals

SumUp Air / Solo

Bluetooth / WiFi

Low hardware cost, simple setup — good for smaller shops.

Verifone P400 / M400

Ethernet / WiFi

Stationary terminal. Most common in traditional registers.

Verifone e285 (Adyen)

WiFi

Handheld terminal with Adyen as acquirer.

Ingenico Move 3500/5000

WiFi / 4G

Mobile terminal from Nets.

Zettle Reader 2

Bluetooth

Quick to get running — good as a backup terminal.

Adyen NYC1

Bluetooth (BLE)

Long battery life, handheld.

Scanners

Zebra DS9208 / DS9308

USB

2D counter scanner. High speed; requires Star mC-Print3 when using USB pass-through.

Zebra CS4070 / CS6080

Bluetooth 2D

For mobile use — e.g. on the sales floor.

Socket Mobile S700 / S820

Bluetooth

S700 for the counter, S820 for mobile use.

Other

Cash drawer (APG Entry Level)

Connects through the printer. No direct connection to the iPad needed.

Customer display Star SCD222

Requires Star mC-Print3. USB connection.

Label printer Zebra ZD421 / GK420

Used with the ExtendaGO app for price labels.

Configure printers & terminals Go to Backoffice → Settings → Printer or Settings → Terminals to connect the equipment. Pick the right connection type (Bluetooth, WiFi, SumUp).
