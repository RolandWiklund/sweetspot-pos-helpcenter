Follow the steps below the first time you set up a register. 1. Download the app 1 Open the App Store on your iPad or iPhone. 2 Search for ExtendaGO POS and install the app. (Sweetspot POS runs on top of the ExtendaGO POS app.) 3 Wait for the download to finish, then open the app. 2. Sign in 1 Enter the sign-in credentials you received from Sweetspot when your account was set up. 2 Pick the correct store (if you have more than one). 3 Pick the correct register — each iPad is usually tied to a specific register. 3. Pick a cashier and enter the float 1 Pick the signed-in cashier's name (or enter a PIN code if your setup requires it). 2 Enter the cash float — the amount in the cash drawer at the start of the day. 3 Confirm, and you'll land on the sales view. TIP If the app shows a red status indicator next to the printer or terminal icon in the top menu — go to Settings → Printer or Settings → Terminals and connect the right equipment. See Hardware. OVERVIEW How the sales view is laid out Once signed in, you land on the sales view (Sales window). Here are the most important elements: Element Function Backoffice (top left) Opens the POS admin view (sales stats, products, stock, settings). Sync icon Shows whether the register is online and synced with the cloud. Terminal status Shows whether the payment terminal is connected. Profile Shows the signed-in cashier. Sign the user out from here. Open cash drawer Opens the physical cash drawer without starting a sale. Search bar Search for products in your catalogue. Product and category grid Your products sorted into categories. Receipt view (right) Shows products added to the current sale. Discount Applies a discount on the selected product or the whole sale. Return Activates return mode (products are added as negative lines). Manual line Adds a line with a free-form price, for products not in the catalogue. Park sale Saves the sale so you can pick it up again later. Cancel / Pay Red button cancels the sale; green button proceeds to payment. AT THE POS Selling products and taking payment Add products to the receipt 1 Tap a product in the product grid, or search for it in the search bar. Alternatively — scan the barcode. 2 If the product has variants (size, color, etc.) — pick the correct variant. 3 The product is added to the receipt view on the right. Tap it there to change quantity, discount or variant. Add a manual product If a customer wants to buy something that isn't in the catalogue, tap manual line and enter a description, price and VAT. Perfect for ad-hoc or unique items. Take payment 1 Tap the green Pay button. 2 Pick a payment method — card, cash, gift card, invoice, MobilePay and more (depending on your setup). 3 Follow the prompt for the chosen method. For card payments, the amount is sent to the card terminal. 4 The receipt is printed automatically on the receipt printer once payment is approved. Park an ongoing sale If a customer needs to wait (e.g. grab something from the car), tap the Park button. You can now serve the next customer. Pick the parked sale back up from the parked-sales list. Split a bill In hospitality/restaurant setups you can split a sale across several people. Tap Share bill and select which lines go on each part. AT THE POS Apply a discount You can add a discount to a single product or to the entire receipt. Discount on a single product 1 Tap the product in the receipt view. 2 Pick percentage discount, amount discount, or set a new price. 3 Enter the value and (optionally) a reason for the discount. 4 Tap OK — the discount appears immediately in the receipt view. Discount on the whole receipt 1 Tap the Discount button in the toolbar. 2 Pick a discount type and value. 3 The discount is applied to every eligible line. GOOD TO KNOW If a discount comes from a campaign (marked as Markdown), it can't be edited or removed at the register. Campaigns are managed in the web backoffice. RECEIPTS Receipts and receipt copies Reprint a receipt for an earlier sale The most common way to get a receipt copy directly from the register. 1 Tap the Backoffice button in the top-left corner of the sales view. 2 Go to Sale in the menu on the left. 3 Pick the Orders tab. All completed sales are listed, sorted by date. Use the Date button in the top right to filter by period. 4 Tap the order you want to print — you'll see what was sold and how it was paid. 5 Tap the printer icon to print a receipt copy, or the envelope icon to email it. TIP If the printer icon doesn't appear — check that the cashier's role has permission to view sales history under Users → Permissions. Send the receipt by email or SMS At the payment screen you can enter an email address or phone number before completing the sale. The same can be done after the fact via Backoffice → Sale → Orders. Pro forma receipt Need to print a preliminary receipt (e.g. for a restaurant table that hasn't paid yet)? Use the Print bill button. This is a supporting document only — not a receipt. Cached receipts (printer offline) If the receipt printer is offline when a sale is closed, the receipt is saved to a print queue. Once the printer is back, a red badge appears on the printer icon — tap the icon and choose Print cached receipts. AT THE POS Returns and refunds Return against a receipt (recommended) 1 Tap the Return button (products in the receipt view now appear in red = return mode). 2 Scan the QR code on the customer's receipt, or type in the receipt number manually. 3 Pick the lines to return. 4 Fill in the Reason for return. 5 Tap Tender and pick how the customer should be refunded. Manual return (no receipt) 1 Tap Return. 2 Add the product being returned — or tap manual line if the product isn't in the catalogue. 3 Enter a reason for the return. 4 Complete the refund payment (cash or refund to card). IMPORTANT While in return mode, all prices show as negative. Tap the Return button again to exit return mode before starting a new regular sale. AT THE POS End of Day (EOD) At the end of the working day you reconcile the register — count cash, bank the deposit and confirm the totals. 1 Tap the EOD button in the sales view. 2 Count the cash in the drawer and enter the amount in Cash in register. 3 Enter the amount going to the bank under Deposit to bank. 4 Confirm the card payment total from the terminal's end-of-day report. 5 Tap Confirm — the system calculates whether it's a balance match or balance mismatch. TIP On a balance mismatch — double-check your count, and look for any untracked cash withdrawals or deposits during the day. The system logs the difference for audit purposes. X-report (snapshot during the day) You can pull an X-report at any time via Backoffice → Reports. It shows sales, returns, discounts and cash status up to the current moment without closing the day. ADMIN Backoffice in the POS The backoffice inside the POS app lets you manage products, stock, users and settings directly on the iPad. Many of the same functions also live in the web backoffice (wbo-etail.wallmob.com). Products & categories Add, edit and remove articles. → Stock management Top up, remove and count inventory. → Users Roles, PIN codes and permissions. → Reports X-reports and sales stats. → Navigating to Backoffice 1 Tap the Backoffice button in the top-left corner of the sales view. 2 You land on a menu view with every admin function: Home, Sale, KPI, Inventory, Stock, Reports, Users, Settings. 3 Tap the Sales window button at the top to go back to the register. ADMIN Products and categories Add a new product 1 Go to Backoffice → Inventory → Products and tap the plus icon (+). 2 Enter a name, description, purchase price excl. VAT and sales price incl. VAT. 3 Enter the stock level. 4 (Optional) Add variants — e.g. size or color. 5 (Optional) Assign the product to one or more categories under Add categories. 6 (Optional) Add a product image. 7 Tap Add product. Edit or delete a product Tap the pencil icon to the right of a product to open edit mode. To delete the product entirely, tap the red Remove button. NOTE When you delete a product, it disappears from every store that had it in the catalogue. If the product has sales history, we recommend archiving it instead. Categories Create categories via Backoffice → Inventory → Categories → Add category. Categories can be multi-level — pick an existing category as the Top-category when creating a sub-category. ADMIN Stock management Add to stock 1 Go to Backoffice → Stock → Add to stock. 2 Tap the plus icon, pick a category and product. 3 Tap the pencil icon and enter the quantity to add. 4 Confirm. You can enter a Reference ID and description (e.g. a supplier invoice number). Remove from stock Same procedure under the Remove from stock tab. Use it for shrinkage or internal consumption. Rolling count (single item) Under Count stock you can count a single product and set the actual balance. Good for ongoing spot checks during the day. Full inventory count 1 Go to Stocks → Stock in the GO CTRL app and pick the right stock location. 2 Go to Inventory counts and tap Open a new, full item count. 3 Pick store and currency, then tap Create item count. 4 Each team member signs in on their device and picks Full items count → Participate in a full item count. 5 Split responsibility between shelves/areas so everything gets counted exactly once. 6 Scan or search each article and enter the quantity. 7 When everyone is done — swipe left on the count and tap Send, confirm with Submit and close. 8 Open the web backoffice → Item count → Products. Check counted vs. uncounted products. 9 Tap Update item count, then go to Details and tap Close. NOTE Counting while the store is open isn't supported by the automation. If you count mid-day, follow up with a rolling count and pull a sales report for the period. ADMIN Users and permissions Manage who can do what at the register. There are three roles by default: Management, Owner and Staff. Add a user 1 Go to Backoffice → Users → Add user. 2 Enter a name and email address. 3 Pick a role via Attach role. 4 Recommended: set a PIN code for quick sign-in at the register. 5 (Optional) Add a profile picture. 6 Tap Add user. Edit what a role can do Go to Users → Permission. Here you can set Staff so they can't process returns, view sales history, or edit products. TIP Cashiers who often get asked for receipt copies need permission to view Orders/Sales history. Check this first when troubleshooting. ADMIN Reports X-report An X-report is a snapshot of the day's sales and cash status without closing the day. Includes among other things: • Products sold • Products returned • Cash drawer openings • Cash in register • Discount totals Pulled via Backoffice → Reports → X-reports and can be printed on the receipt printer. Sales stats Under Home → Sale you'll find revenue per hour, day, week and month as charts. Filter by any period with the Date button. KPI Under KPI you'll find the average basket size and margin per product — handy for quickly spotting what's making the most money. HARDWARE Recommended hardware Sweetspot POS works with a wide range of equipment. Here's what we recommend — reach out to support if you're unsure what fits your setup. iPad and iPhone iPad iPadOS 13 or later All modern iPads work — a newer model gives the best performance, especially when scanning and taking payment simultaneously. iPhone iOS 13 or later Used for mobile POS and for the ExtendaGO CTRL app (stock, counts). Receipt printers Star mC-Print3 LAN + USB hub The standard choice. Supports cash drawer, scanner and customer display from a single unit. Star mPOP Printer + cash drawer Combined unit. Compact option for smaller registers. Epson TM-T88VI BT / LAN / WiFi Fast printer (300mm/s) for high-volume stores. Star TSP143IIIU USB Budget-friendly — doesn't support every scanner though. Seiko RP-F10 USB / USB-C / BT Compact design, IPx1 splash rating on some models. Card terminals Sweetspot POS uses Adyen as payment acquirer. The following Adyen terminals are supported. Adyen V400m Ethernet / WiFi Stationary countertop terminal. The most common choice for traditional registers. Adyen V400C WiFi Compact stationary terminal — fits tight counter spaces. Adyen NYC1 Bluetooth (BLE) Handheld cordless terminal with long battery life. Great for mobile use on the floor. Scanners Zebra DS9208 / DS9308 USB 2D counter scanner. High speed; requires Star mC-Print3 when using USB pass-through. Zebra CS4070 / CS6080 Bluetooth 2D For mobile use — e.g. on the sales floor. Socket Mobile S700 / S820 Bluetooth S700 for the counter, S820 for mobile use. Other Cash drawer (APG Entry Level) Connects through the printer. No direct connection to the iPad needed. Customer display Star SCD222 Requires Star mC-Print3. USB connection. Label printer Zebra ZD421 / GK420 Used with the ExtendaGO app for price labels. CONFIGURE PRINTERS & TERMINALS Go to BACKOFFICE → SETTINGS → PRINTER or SETTINGS → TERMINALS to connect the equipment. Pick the right connection type (Bluetooth or WiFi) for your Adyen terminal.
